## Description (4000 chars max)

My Words is an Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) app designed for non-verbal individuals, including those with autism, apraxia, cerebral palsy, or any condition that makes spoken communication difficult. It turns an iPhone or iPad into a powerful voice output communication aid.

**How It Works**
Tap any picture button and the app speaks the word or phrase out loud using natural-sounding AI voices. Large, colorful buttons are easy to see and tap — even for users with fine motor challenges.

**Natural AI Voices**
My Words uses advanced AI text-to-speech so communication sounds natural and expressive, not robotic. Choose from multiple voices across two AI providers, or use the built-in iOS voice for offline use:
• Groq Orpheus voices: Autumn, Diana, Hannah, Austin, Daniel, Troy
• OpenAI voices: Alloy, Echo, Fable, Onyx, Nova, Shimmer
• System voice: works completely offline

**Fully Customizable**
Every button can be personalized to match the user's daily needs:
• Add new buttons with any photo from your camera roll
• Edit the label, spoken phrase, and background color of any button
• Replace the default pictures with photos of real objects from your home
• Delete buttons that aren't needed
• Rearrange buttons to put the most-used ones first

**Designed for Accessibility**
• Extra-large buttons fill the screen for easy tapping
• High-contrast colored borders make each button distinct
• Visual feedback when a button is speaking
• Works on both iPhone and iPad
• Supports portrait and landscape orientations

**Privacy First**
My Words does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Photos you add stay on your device. Voice requests are processed securely and not stored by the AI providers.

My Words is built with love for families, caregivers, teachers, and therapists who support non-verbal individuals every day.

## App Review Notes

**No account or login required** — the app works immediately on launch.

**Testing the app:**
1. Tap any of the 4 default picture buttons (Milk, Time to Go, Brush Teeth, Potty) — the device will speak the label aloud using AI text-to-speech.
2. To add a new button: tap the blue "Add Button" at the bottom right.
3. To edit or delete a button: tap "Edit" in the top left, then tap the pencil (edit) or X (delete) icon on any button.
4. To change the voice: tap the gear icon (top right) → choose a provider and voice → tap "Preview Voice" to hear it.

**TTS Notes for Reviewers:**
The app uses Groq and OpenAI APIs for AI voices. API keys are embedded in the build. If AI voices fail (network required), the app automatically falls back to the iOS system voice — so TTS always works even without internet.

**Permissions used:**
• Photo Library — only requested when the user taps "Add Button" or "Replace with Photo" to choose a custom picture. No photos are accessed without user action.
